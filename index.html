Important — Read Before Outreach
These are NOT Job Listings — They are Prospecting Targets
Strategy guide on how to approach each lead group correctly
Group 1
🦷 Dental Clinics & MedSpas
What they are: Business owners who likely run their own ads poorly (or not at all), or pay an expensive agency. They have high-ticket services and real urgency to fill appointment slots.
💬 Your Pitch Angle
"I can run your Meta/Google ads and get you more booked appointments at better ROAS than what you're doing now."
Group 2
📊 Marketing Agencies (US/CA/AU)
What they are: Agencies that manage ads for many clients and often need extra media buying capacity. SmartSites, Brill Media, Anderson Collaborative, Power Digital, MuteSix, etc.
💬 Your Pitch Angle
Offer white-label media buying — you run the ads under their brand. This is a very common and scalable model. Some may also have open roles on their careers pages.
Group 3
🇵🇭 Philippines Agencies
What they are: Local PH agencies growing fast — SwingUp, Spiralytics, LeapOut, Huskee Digital, Gatchi — many serving US/AU clients and actively expanding their teams.
💬 Your Pitch Angle
Pitch as a contractor/white-label partner OR apply directly. PH agencies often hire media buyers locally and are a strong employment target.
